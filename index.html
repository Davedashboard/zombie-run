ZOMBIE RUN

The undead are shambling down the woodland trails. Hold the line!
How to play: tap a tower in the bottom bar, then tap a glowing spot beside the trail to build it. Tap a built tower to upgrade it (up to level 4) or sell it. Press ▶ Wave to send in the zombies. Every zombie that reaches your base costs lives (bosses cost 5!). Survive every wave to win. 20 levels across 5 worlds, and each one is tougher than the last. ⏩ speeds things up.
